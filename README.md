
# Bakery Retail Business Analysis

An Excel-based business analysis project focused on retail sales performance, product profitability, inventory efficiency, and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Overview

This project analyses a bakery retail dataset to understand sales trends, compare store and category performance, identify products with high unsold quantities, and develop recommendations to support retail planning and business review meetings.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse sales and profitability across product categories and stores.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
- Examine unsold inventory and production efficiency.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends.
- Develop recommendations for improving inventory planning.
- Organise follow-up actions for Business Review Meetings (BRMs).

## Tools and Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel tables and formulas
- Conditional formatting and charts
- Dashboard reporting
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Sales and profitability analysis
- Inventory analysis
- Business reporting

## Dataset

- **Records:** 19,321
- **Columns:** 40
- **Period:** January 2023 – December 2024
- **Domain:** Bakery retail sales and inventory

The dataset contains sales transactions, product categories, stores, production quantities, unsold units, customer attributes, promotions, and profitability information.

**Note:** This is a third-party dataset, not V-Mart internal data. Refer to the original dataset page for attribution and licensing terms.

## Key Metrics

- Total sales
- Total recorded profit
- Units sold and produced
- Unsold units and unsold rate
- Profit margin
- Category and store performance

## Initial Findings

- **Total recorded sales:** ₹26.91 lakh
- **Total recorded profit:** ₹11.44 lakh
- **Units produced:** 81,563
- **Unsold units:** 22,696
- **Unsold share of production:** 27.8%

These metrics provide a starting point for investigating product demand and production planning. Unsold quantities should be interpreted in the context of the source dataset's definitions.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate products with consistently high unsold rates.
2. Compare category-level sales and profitability to inform product-mix decisions.
3. Investigate differences in store performance before recommending operational changes.
4. Examine monthly trends for potential seasonal patterns.
5. Track follow-up actions with clear owners and deadlines.

These recommendations are areas for investigation, not proven causal conclusions.

## Limitations

- The dataset does not contain budget or sales targets, so budget-versus-actual analysis is not included.
- The source dataset contains missing values, which are documented in the workbook.
- The analysis is descriptive; potential causes of performance differences require further validation.
- Recorded sales and profit figures depend on the source dataset's definitions.
